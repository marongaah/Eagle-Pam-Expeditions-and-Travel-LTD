TOUR OVERVIEW

9-Day Kenya & Tanzania Wildlife Safari

This 9-day East Africa safari begins and ends in Nairobi, combining Kenya's famous Masai Mara with Tanzania's legendary Serengeti National Park and Ngorongoro Conservation Area.

Begin in Nairobi with visits to the David Sheldrick Elephant Orphanage, Giraffe Centre and Karen Blixen Museum. Continue to the Masai Mara, where you will experience a full-day game drive and a visit to a traditional Maasai community project.

Cross into Tanzania for game viewing in the Serengeti, then travel to the Ngorongoro Crater for a 4WD excursion on the crater floor and opportunities to look for the Big Five. The journey concludes with a return transfer to Nairobi.

DAY BY DAY

9-Day East Africa Safari Itinerary

Travel from Nairobi through Kenya's Masai Mara and into Tanzania's Serengeti and Ngorongoro region before returning to Nairobi.

Day 1

Arrival / Nairobi

Begin your East Africa adventure in Nairobi. The listed start point is Nairobi Central, with hotel pick-up within Nairobi between 07:30 and 08:00.

  • Meet-up and pick-up at a Nairobi hotel
  • Breakfast
  • Prepare for the safari journey

Meals: Breakfast

Day 2

Nairobi

Discover some of Nairobi's best-known wildlife and heritage attractions before enjoying dinner at the famous Carnivore restaurant.

  • Visit David Sheldrick Elephant Orphanage
  • Visit Giraffe Centre
  • Visit Karen Blixen Museum
  • Dinner at Carnivore restaurant

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Day 3

Nairobi → Masai Mara

Travel from Nairobi towards the Masai Mara, with a cultural visit to Tepesua Village. Meet Maasai community members and learn about local traditions and the Tepesua Widows Income Project.

  • Visit Tepesua Village
  • Meet Maasai warriors with traditional song and dance
  • Tour a traditional Maasai home and cattle enclosures
  • Learn about the Tepesua Widows Income Project
  • Landmarks include Tepesua Village and Loita Hills

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Day 4

Masai Mara

Spend a full day exploring the Masai Mara National Reserve on a game drive, with a picnic lunch at the Mara River.

  • Full-day game drive in Masai Mara National Reserve
  • Picnic lunch at Mara River
  • Optional sunrise hot air balloon safari — US$450 per person

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Day 5

Masai Mara → Serengeti

Continue across the Kenya–Tanzania safari route towards the Serengeti. The day includes a game drive towards the Serengeti campsite and a picnic lunch at the Serengeti entrance.

  • Travel from Masai Mara to Serengeti
  • Game drive en route to the Serengeti campsite
  • Picnic lunch at Serengeti entrance
  • Arrive at the Serengeti campsite

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Day 6

Serengeti

Enjoy morning and afternoon game drives in the Serengeti, giving you more time to explore its wildlife-rich plains.

  • Early morning game drive in Serengeti
  • Afternoon game drive in Serengeti
  • Wildlife viewing across the national park

Meals: Breakfast, Brunch and Dinner

Day 7

Serengeti → Ngorongoro

Enjoy a final game drive as you exit the Serengeti, then continue towards the Ngorongoro Conservation Area and its famous crater rim.

  • Final game drive exiting Serengeti
  • Travel to Ngorongoro
  • Visit Ngorongoro Crater rim

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Day 8

Ngorongoro Crater → Arusha

Descend into the Ngorongoro Crater for a 4WD excursion on the crater floor. Look out for the Big Five and other wildlife before continuing to Arusha.

  • 4WD excursion on Ngorongoro Crater floor
  • Big Five wildlife viewing
  • Optional group dinner celebration in Arusha
  • Optional Olduvai Gorge Museum visit — US$45 per person

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Day 9

Arusha → Nairobi

Conclude your East Africa safari with the return journey to Nairobi. The listed end point is Nairobi Central, with hotel drop-off between 16:00 and 17:00.

  • Return transfer from Arusha to Nairobi
  • Optional departure transfer to Kilimanjaro Airport (JRO)
  • Optional departure transfer to Arusha Airport (ARK)
  • Hotel drop-off in Nairobi

Meals: Breakfast
