DAY BY DAY

Safari Itinerary

Discover Kenya's most iconic wildlife destinations on this carefully planned seven-day safari through the Masai Mara, Lake Nakuru, Lake Naivasha and Amboseli National Park.

Day 1

Nairobi → Masai Mara National Reserve

Your safari begins with an early morning pickup from your Nairobi hotel or the airport before travelling through the spectacular Great Rift Valley. Stop at the famous viewpoint before continuing to the world-renowned Masai Mara National Reserve. Arrive in time for lunch followed by your first exciting afternoon game drive.

  • Pickup from Nairobi Hotel or Airport
  • Great Rift Valley viewpoint stop
  • Drive to Masai Mara National Reserve
  • Lunch at the safari lodge
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner & overnight stay
Day 2

Full Day in Masai Mara

Spend the entire day exploring the endless plains of the Masai Mara searching for the legendary Big Five. Visit the Mara River during migration season and enjoy a picnic lunch surrounded by Africa's incredible wildlife.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Big Five wildlife viewing
  • Mara River visit
  • Picnic lunch inside the reserve
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner & overnight stay
Day 3

Masai Mara → Lake Nakuru

After breakfast depart the Masai Mara and journey toward Lake Nakuru. Enjoy scenic views of the Kenyan countryside, passing Lake Naivasha and Mount Longonot. Optional visits to a traditional Maasai Village can be arranged along the way.

  • Breakfast at the lodge
  • Depart Masai Mara
  • Scenic drive through the Rift Valley
  • Optional Maasai Village visit
  • Arrival in Nakuru
  • Dinner & overnight
Day 4

Lake Nakuru → Lake Naivasha

Begin the morning with a thrilling game drive inside Lake Nakuru National Park, famous for its rhino sanctuary, Rothschild giraffes and diverse birdlife. Afterwards, continue to the beautiful shores of Lake Naivasha where you'll relax for the evening.

  • Morning game drive
  • Rhino tracking
  • Bird watching
  • Drive to Lake Naivasha
  • Relax at the lodge
  • Dinner & overnight
Day 5

Lake Naivasha → Hell's Gate → Amboseli National Park

After breakfast, visit the spectacular Hell's Gate National Park where you'll enjoy a guided walk among dramatic cliffs, gorges and fascinating geothermal features. Later, continue south towards Amboseli National Park, arriving in the evening with magnificent views of Mount Kilimanjaro.

  • Breakfast at the lodge
  • Visit Hell's Gate National Park
  • Guided nature walk
  • Scenic drive to Amboseli
  • Mount Kilimanjaro views
  • Dinner & overnight stay
Day 6

Full Day Game Drive in Amboseli National Park

Spend the entire day exploring Amboseli National Park, famous for its large elephant herds and breathtaking backdrop of Mount Kilimanjaro. Enjoy morning and afternoon game drives in search of elephants, lions, cheetahs, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras and many other wildlife species.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Elephant photography
  • Big game viewing
  • Picnic lunch inside the park
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner & overnight stay
Day 7

Amboseli National Park → Nairobi

Wake up early for one final game drive as the African wilderness comes alive. After breakfast, check out and begin the journey back to Nairobi, where you'll be dropped off at your hotel or Jomo Kenyatta International Airport, marking the end of your unforgettable Kenyan safari.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Breakfast at the lodge
  • Depart Amboseli
  • Scenic drive to Nairobi
  • Hotel/Airport drop-off
  • End of Safari

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

What is the best time to take this safari?

This safari operates throughout the year. July to October is ideal for witnessing the Great Migration in the Masai Mara, while Amboseli offers spectacular elephant viewing and Mount Kilimanjaro scenery all year round.

Which wildlife can I expect to see?

You'll have excellent chances of spotting the Big Five, giraffes, zebras, cheetahs, hippos, crocodiles, flamingos, hyenas, numerous antelope species and hundreds of bird species across the four destinations.

Is this a private safari?

Yes. This itinerary is operated as a private safari with your own professional driver-guide and dedicated 4x4 Land Cruiser, allowing maximum flexibility throughout your journey.

Are park entrance fees included?

Yes. All national park entrance fees listed in the itinerary are included in the safari price, so there are no hidden conservation charges during your trip.

Can this itinerary be customized?

Absolutely. Eagle Pam Expeditions can customize accommodation levels, safari duration, destinations, activities and departure dates to match your preferences and budget.
