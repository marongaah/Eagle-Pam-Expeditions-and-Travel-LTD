TOUR OVERVIEW

5 Days Kenya Budget Jeep Safari

This 5-day budget jeep safari starts and ends in Nairobi and packs some of Kenya's best-loved wildlife and scenery into a short, affordable journey.

Spend two days on game drives in the Masai Mara, then travel via Mount Longonot and Lake Naivasha to Lake Nakuru National Park, before a walk on Crescent Island and a nature walk in Hell's Gate National Park.

Travel with an experienced English-speaking guide in a small group of 2 to 7 travellers, with game drives, scenic stops and a relaxed pace throughout.

DAY BY DAY

5-Day Kenya Budget Jeep Safari Itinerary

Start and end in Nairobi while visiting the Masai Mara, Lake Nakuru, Lake Naivasha and Hell's Gate on a budget-friendly jeep safari.

Day 1

Nairobi → Masai Mara Game Reserve

Your driver picks you up from your Nairobi hotel at around 8:00 a.m. for the drive to the Masai Mara, passing the Great Rift Valley and stopping in Narok town for lunch. Arrive at camp and head out for your first game drive in search of animals and birds.

  • Hotel pick-up in Nairobi
  • Great Rift Valley views
  • Lunch in Narok town
  • Evening game drive
  • Dinner and overnight at camp
Day 2

Full Day Masai Mara Game Reserve

Spend the full day on game viewing across the Masai Mara, one of Africa's greatest wildlife destinations, with a picnic lunch at the hippo pool.

  • Full day game viewing
  • Picnic lunch at the hippo pool
  • Hippo and crocodile viewing
  • Dinner and overnight at camp
Day 3

Masai Mara → Nakuru

Begin with an early morning game drive in the Masai Mara before breakfast. Then travel towards Nakuru with lunch along the way, passing Mount Longonot and Lake Naivasha.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Breakfast and departure
  • Views of Mount Longonot and Lake Naivasha
  • Lunch en route
  • Dinner and overnight in Nakuru
Day 4

Nakuru → Naivasha

Enjoy a game drive in Lake Nakuru National Park, then continue to Lake Naivasha for a hot lunch and an evening walk on Crescent Island. A boat ride on Lake Naivasha is available as an optional extra.

  • Lake Nakuru game drive
  • Hot lunch
  • Evening walk on Crescent Island
  • Optional boat ride on Lake Naivasha (USD $25 per person)
  • Dinner and overnight in Naivasha
Day 5

Naivasha → Nairobi (Hell's Gate National Park)

After breakfast, enjoy a nature walk in Hell's Gate National Park, with optional bicycle rental available. After a hot lunch, return to Nairobi and be dropped off at your hotel in the late afternoon.

  • Breakfast
  • Nature walk in Hell's Gate National Park
  • Optional bicycle rental (USD $10 per person)
  • Hot lunch
  • Drop-off at Nairobi hotels (17:00 - 17:30)
