DAY BY DAY

12-Day Tanzania & Kenya Safari Itinerary

Start and end in Nairobi while visiting the Masai Mara, Lake Nakuru, Amboseli, Tarangire, Ngorongoro and the Serengeti on a camping safari packed with game drives.

Day 1

Nairobi → Masai Mara Game Reserve

Your driver picks you up from your Nairobi hotel between 7:00 and 8:00 a.m. for the drive to the Masai Mara. Stop at the Great Rift Valley viewpoint, continue through Narok town for refuelling and lunch, and reach camp by mid-afternoon in time for your first game viewing.

  • Hotel pick-up in Nairobi
  • Great Rift Valley viewpoint
  • Lunch in Narok town
  • Evening game viewing
  • Dinner and overnight at camp
Day 2

Full Day Masai Mara Game Reserve

After breakfast, spend the full day on game drives across the reserve's savannah grasslands and rolling hills, with plenty of chances for close-up wildlife viewing and photography.

  • Full day game viewing
  • Picnic lunch at the hippo pool
  • Hippo and crocodile viewing
  • Dinner and overnight at camp
Day 3

Masai Mara → Nakuru

Start with an early morning game drive before breakfast, when wildlife is most active. Then head towards Nakuru with lunch along the way, passing views of Mt. Longonot and Lake Naivasha from the highway.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Breakfast and departure
  • Views of Mt. Longonot and Lake Naivasha
  • Dinner and overnight at a budget hotel
Day 4

Nakuru → Amboseli National Park

After breakfast, enjoy a game drive through Lake Nakuru National Park, known for its birdlife, flamingos and white rhino conservation. Then drive to Amboseli with more game viewing along the way.

  • Lake Nakuru game drive
  • Flamingos and white rhino
  • Drive to Amboseli
  • Dinner and overnight at camp
Day 5

Full Day Amboseli National Park

Spend the day on game drives around Amboseli's swamps, where elephants and hippos come to bathe, alongside plains game, antelopes and birdlife. If the weather cooperates, Mt. Kilimanjaro's peak comes into view.

  • Game drives in Amboseli
  • Elephant and hippo viewing
  • Views of Mt. Kilimanjaro
  • Picnic lunch
  • Dinner and overnight at camp
Day 6

Amboseli → Arusha

Begin with an early morning game drive, then return for breakfast and check-out. Drive to the Namanga border, complete immigration, and take a shuttle bus to Arusha where your guide meets you and transfers you to Arusha Charity Hotel.

  • Early morning game viewing
  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Namanga border crossing
  • Shuttle to Arusha
  • Overnight at Arusha Charity Hotel
Day 7

Arusha → Tarangire National Park

After breakfast, depart for Tarangire National Park, arriving in time for lunch and an afternoon game drive. The park is filled with baobab trees and famous for its tree-climbing lions.

  • Breakfast and departure
  • Lunch
  • Afternoon game viewing
  • Dinner and overnight at the campsite
Day 8

Tarangire → Ngorongoro Conservation Area

Following breakfast, drive to the Ngorongoro Conservation Area and descend about 2,000 feet onto the crater floor for an extensive crater tour through forest, flamingo lake and open savannah where lions hunt.

  • Breakfast and drive to Ngorongoro
  • Crater descent and game drive
  • Picnic lunch
  • Dinner and overnight at camp
Day 9

Ngorongoro → Serengeti National Park

Head to the Serengeti by way of Olduvai Gorge, where the Leakeys made major archaeological discoveries. After lunch at Seronera camp, enjoy an afternoon game drive across the vast plains in search of wildebeest, zebras, gazelles and lions.

  • Drive via Olduvai Gorge
  • Optional Olduvai museum visit (extra cost)
  • Lunch at Seronera camp
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight at the campsite
Day 10

Full Day Serengeti National Park

After breakfast in camp, set out with picnic lunches for a full day tracking wildlife across the savannah grasslands, continuing game drives until evening.

  • Full day game drive
  • Picnic lunch
  • Wildlife and predator viewing
  • Dinner and overnight at the campsite
Day 11

Serengeti → Arusha

Enjoy an early morning game drive before returning to camp for a late breakfast. After a mid-morning drive, head back to Arusha for the night at Charity Hotel.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Late breakfast at camp
  • Drive back to Arusha
  • Overnight at Arusha Charity Hotel
Day 12

Arusha → Nairobi

Following breakfast, board the scheduled morning shuttle bus for your return to Nairobi, arriving at the airport between 5:00 and 5:30 p.m.

  • Breakfast
  • Morning shuttle bus to Nairobi
  • Airport drop-off (17:00 - 17:30)
