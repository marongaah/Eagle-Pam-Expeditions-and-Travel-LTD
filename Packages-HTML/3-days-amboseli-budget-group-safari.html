DAY BY DAY

3-Day Amboseli Safari Itinerary

Experience one of Kenya's most iconic wildlife destinations on this exciting 3-day safari featuring spectacular elephant herds, breathtaking views of Mount Kilimanjaro and unforgettable game drives through Amboseli National Park.

Day 1

Nairobi → Amboseli National Park

Depart Nairobi early in the morning and travel south through the beautiful Kenyan countryside to Amboseli National Park. Arrive in time for lunch before enjoying your first afternoon game drive in search of elephants, lions, giraffes, zebras and many other wildlife species beneath the magnificent Mount Kilimanjaro.

  • Early morning departure from Nairobi
  • Scenic drive to Amboseli National Park
  • Lunch and check-in at your lodge or camp
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 2

Full Day Game Drive in Amboseli

Spend the entire day exploring Amboseli National Park. Enjoy morning and afternoon game drives while searching for Africa's famous wildlife including large elephant herds, buffaloes, hyenas, cheetahs, lions and numerous bird species. Weather permitting, enjoy spectacular views of snow-capped Mount Kilimanjaro.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Visit Observation Hill
  • Picnic lunch or lodge lunch
  • Afternoon wildlife safari
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 3

Amboseli → Nairobi

Enjoy an early morning game drive and breakfast before checking out. Depart Amboseli and journey back to Nairobi, arriving in the afternoon with wonderful memories of Kenya's famous elephant paradise.

  • Optional sunrise game drive
  • Breakfast at the lodge
  • Check-out and departure
  • Drive back to Nairobi
  • Drop-off at hotel or airport

QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I choose between Budget, Mid-range and Luxury accommodation?

Yes. This safari is available in Budget, Mid-range and Luxury options. The itinerary remains the same while the accommodation standard changes to suit your travel style and budget.

What animals can I expect to see?

Amboseli National Park is famous for its large elephant herds, lions, cheetahs, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras, wildebeest, hyenas, gazelles and over 400 bird species. You'll also enjoy spectacular views of Mount Kilimanjaro on clear days.

Is this safari suitable for families?

Absolutely. This safari is ideal for couples, families, solo travellers and small groups looking for a short but unforgettable wildlife experience.
