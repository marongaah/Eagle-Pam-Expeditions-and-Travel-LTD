TOUR OVERVIEW

Explore Samburu National Reserve

Experience the wild beauty of northern Kenya on this exciting 3-day Samburu safari. Samburu National Reserve is famous for its dramatic landscapes, rugged hills, the Ewaso Ng'iro River and remarkable wildlife found nowhere else in Kenya.

Travel in a comfortable 4x4 Land Cruiser with an experienced safari guide while enjoying exciting game drives through the reserve. Look out for Samburu's famous Special Five — Grevy's zebra, reticulated giraffe, Somali ostrich, gerenuk and Beisa oryx.

This budget group safari offers an excellent opportunity to experience one of Kenya's most unique wildlife destinations while staying at comfortable accommodation at an affordable price.

DAY BY DAY

3-Day Samburu Safari Itinerary

Explore the spectacular landscapes of Samburu National Reserve on this exciting 3-day safari. Enjoy thrilling game drives, encounter Samburu's unique northern wildlife and experience the beauty of Kenya's rugged northern frontier.

Day 1

Nairobi → Samburu National Reserve

Depart Nairobi early in the morning and travel north through the changing landscapes of central Kenya towards Samburu National Reserve. Enjoy scenic views along the way before arriving at your lodge in time for lunch. After check-in, head out for your first exciting afternoon game drive.

  • Early morning departure from Nairobi
  • Scenic journey through central Kenya
  • Arrival and lunch at the lodge
  • Afternoon game drive in Samburu
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 2

Full Day Game Drive in Samburu

Spend a full day exploring the breathtaking Samburu National Reserve. Enjoy morning and afternoon game drives through the reserve while searching for the region's famous wildlife, including elephants, lions, leopards, giraffes, zebras and the unique Samburu Special Five.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Breakfast at the lodge
  • Full-day wildlife exploration
  • Picnic or lodge lunch
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 3

Samburu → Nairobi

Start the final morning with an optional game drive before breakfast. After breakfast, check out and begin your journey back to Nairobi. Enjoy the changing scenery as you travel south, arriving in Nairobi in the afternoon with unforgettable memories of your Samburu safari.

  • Optional early morning game drive
  • Breakfast at the lodge
  • Check-out and departure
  • Scenic drive back to Nairobi
  • Drop-off at your hotel or airport

QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some of the most common questions about our 3-day Samburu group safari.

Can I choose between Budget, Mid-Range and Luxury accommodation?

Yes. This 3-day Samburu safari is available in Budget, Mid-Range and Luxury accommodation options. The safari itinerary and wildlife experiences remain the same, while the accommodation standard and package price change according to your selected option.

What animals can I expect to see in Samburu?

Samburu National Reserve is famous for its unique northern wildlife, including the Grevy's zebra, reticulated giraffe, gerenuk, Beisa oryx and Somali ostrich. You may also encounter elephants, lions, leopards, cheetahs, buffaloes, warthogs and many different bird species.

Is this a group safari?

Yes. This is a budget-friendly group safari designed for travellers who want to experience Samburu while sharing the safari vehicle and selected services with other travellers. Group sizes may vary depending on the departure date.

Can I join the safari as a solo traveller?

Absolutely. Solo travellers are welcome to join our group departures. This is a great way to explore Samburu while keeping the cost of the safari more affordable.

What type of vehicle is used for the safari?

We use a comfortable 4x4 safari Land Cruiser with a pop-up roof, providing excellent visibility and photography opportunities during game drives.

Can I request a private safari instead?

Yes. If you prefer to travel privately with your family, friends or partner, a private safari can be arranged. The itinerary can also be customized to suit your preferred travel dates and interests.

How do I book the 3-day Samburu safari?

Simply click the booking button on this page and send us your preferred travel dates, number of travellers and accommodation category. Our team will confirm availability and guide you through the booking process.
