DAY BY DAY

Safari Itinerary

Experience Kenya's most famous wildlife destinations on this exciting 6-day budget camping safari through the Masai Mara, Lake Nakuru and Amboseli National Park.

Day 1

Nairobi → Masai Mara National Reserve

Depart Nairobi in the morning and travel through the Great Rift Valley to the world-famous Masai Mara National Reserve. Arrive in time for lunch before enjoying your first exciting afternoon game drive.

  • Morning departure from Nairobi
  • Scenic Great Rift Valley stop
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Overnight at Miti Mingi Camp
Day 2

Full Day in Masai Mara

Spend the entire day exploring the vast plains of the Masai Mara in search of the Big Five and other incredible wildlife. Enjoy a picnic lunch inside the reserve before continuing your afternoon game drive.

  • Morning game drive
  • Big Five wildlife viewing
  • Picnic lunch in the reserve
  • Overnight at Miti Mingi Camp
Day 3

Masai Mara → Lake Nakuru

After breakfast, depart the Masai Mara and journey to Lake Nakuru National Park. Enjoy an afternoon game drive in Kenya's famous rhino sanctuary before checking into your hotel.

  • Morning departure
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Rhino sanctuary visit
  • Overnight at Buraha Zenoni Hotel
Day 4

Lake Nakuru → Amboseli National Park

Leave Lake Nakuru after breakfast and travel south towards Amboseli National Park. Arrive later in the day and settle into camp beneath the breathtaking backdrop of Mount Kilimanjaro.

  • Scenic drive to Amboseli
  • Views of Mount Kilimanjaro
  • Evening relaxation
  • Overnight at Nyati Safari Camp
Day 5

Full Day in Amboseli

Enjoy a full day exploring Amboseli National Park, famous for its large elephant herds, incredible wildlife and spectacular views of Africa's highest mountain, Mount Kilimanjaro.

  • Morning game drive
  • Elephant viewing
  • Excellent photography opportunities
  • Overnight at Nyati Safari Camp
Day 6

Amboseli → Nairobi

After breakfast, enjoy your final views of Amboseli before departing for Nairobi. Arrive in the afternoon with unforgettable memories of Kenya's spectacular wildlife and landscapes.

  • Breakfast at camp
  • Morning departure
  • Drive back to Nairobi
  • End of safari

QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a group camping safari?

Yes. This is a scheduled group camping safari where you travel with other adventurers in a custom-built 4x4 Safari Land Cruiser, making it an affordable way to experience Kenya's top wildlife destinations.

What wildlife can I expect to see?

You'll have excellent opportunities to see the Big Five, including lions, elephants, buffaloes, leopards and rhinos, as well as giraffes, zebras, wildebeest, cheetahs, hyenas, hippos and hundreds of bird species.

What type of accommodation is provided?

Accommodation is provided in comfortable budget safari camps and hotels including Miti Mingi Eco Camp, Buraha Zenoni Hotel and Nyati Safari Camp, or similar properties, all offering excellent hospitality and convenient access to the parks.
