DAY BY DAY

7-Day Kenya & Tanzania Safari Itinerary

Experience an unforgettable cross-border safari through Lake Nakuru, Masai Mara, Serengeti and Ngorongoro, combining spectacular wildlife, scenic landscapes and exciting game drives across Kenya and Tanzania.

Day 1

Nairobi → Lake Nakuru National Park

Depart Nairobi in the morning and travel through the scenic Kenyan countryside toward Lake Nakuru National Park. Arrive in time for lunch before enjoying an afternoon game drive in search of rhinos, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras and other wildlife.

  • Morning departure from Nairobi
  • Scenic drive toward the Great Rift Valley
  • Lunch and check-in
  • Afternoon game drive in Lake Nakuru
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 2

Lake Nakuru → Masai Mara National Reserve

After breakfast, depart Lake Nakuru and travel toward the world-famous Masai Mara National Reserve. Arrive for lunch before heading out on an exciting afternoon game drive across the Mara's wildlife-rich plains.

  • Breakfast at the lodge or camp
  • Departure from Lake Nakuru
  • Scenic journey to Masai Mara
  • Lunch and check-in
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 3

Full Day Masai Mara Game Drive

Spend a full day exploring the Masai Mara National Reserve. Enjoy morning and afternoon game drives while searching for the Big Five and the many other species that make the Mara one of Africa's greatest safari destinations.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Breakfast at the lodge or camp
  • Full-day wildlife exploration
  • Picnic lunch or lodge lunch
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 4

Masai Mara → Serengeti National Park

Following breakfast, you'll travel toward the Kenya-Tanzania border at Sirari with lunch along the way. You'll arrive in the late afternoon or early evening, then take a game drive to see the Serengeti's wide-open plains before dinner and an overnight stay at Serengeti Wild Camp.

  • Breakfast and departure from Masai Mara
  • Travel toward the Sirari Kenya-Tanzania border
  • Border crossing formalities
  • Lunch along the way
  • Enter Tanzania and continue toward Serengeti
  • Late afternoon or early evening game drive
  • Dinner and overnight at Serengeti Wild Camp
Day 5

Full Day Serengeti National Park

Spend a full day exploring the legendary Serengeti. Enjoy morning and afternoon game drives across the endless plains in search of lions, elephants, cheetahs, leopards, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras and other wildlife.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Breakfast at camp
  • Full-day Serengeti wildlife experience
  • Picnic lunch or camp lunch
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 6

Serengeti → Ngorongoro Conservation Area

After breakfast, enjoy a final game drive through the Serengeti as you make your way toward the Ngorongoro Conservation Area. Continue through Tanzania's spectacular highlands before arriving at your campsite for dinner and overnight.

  • Breakfast at the camp
  • Morning game drive
  • Depart Serengeti
  • Scenic journey toward Ngorongoro
  • Arrive at the campsite
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 7

Ngorongoro Crater → Arusha

Enjoy an early breakfast before descending into the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater for an unforgettable morning game drive. Explore the crater floor in search of lions, elephants, buffaloes, rhinos, hippos and other wildlife before ascending and continuing to Arusha, where the safari ends.

  • Early breakfast
  • Descend into Ngorongoro Crater
  • Morning crater game drive
  • Picnic lunch
  • Ascend from the crater
  • Drive to Arusha
  • Safari ends in Arusha

QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your 7-day Kenya and Tanzania safari adventure.

Can I join this safari as a group traveller?

Yes. This safari is designed for travellers looking for an affordable Kenya and Tanzania adventure. You can join a scheduled group safari and share the safari vehicle and selected accommodation with other travellers.

Where does the safari start and end?

The safari starts in Nairobi, Kenya, and continues through Lake Nakuru, the Masai Mara and into Tanzania through the Sirari border. The safari ends in Arusha, Tanzania.

Will we cross the Kenya-Tanzania border?

Yes. The safari crosses from Kenya into Tanzania through the Sirari border. Our team will assist with the border crossing process before continuing toward the Serengeti.

What wildlife can I expect to see?

Depending on the season and wildlife movements, you may encounter elephants, lions, leopards, cheetahs, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras, wildebeest, hippos, hyenas, gazelles and many species of birds. The Masai Mara and Serengeti offer particularly excellent opportunities for wildlife viewing.

Does the safari include the Serengeti and Ngorongoro Crater?

Yes. The itinerary includes game viewing in the Serengeti National Park and a visit to the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater, subject to the itinerary and park regulations.

What type of accommodation will we use?

Accommodation includes Lake Nakuru Sopa Lodge, Mara River Camp, Serengeti Wild Camp and Ngorongoro Wild Camp, or similar properties of the same category depending on availability.

Are meals included during the safari?

Yes. The package includes full-board accommodation, meaning breakfast, lunch and dinner are provided during the safari. Drinking water is also provided during game drives.

What vehicle will we use?

You will travel in a comfortable 4x4 safari Land Cruiser with a professional safari guide. The vehicle is equipped for wildlife viewing and game drives in both Kenya and Tanzania.

Do I need visas for Kenya and Tanzania?

Visa and entry requirements depend on your nationality. Travellers are responsible for obtaining the required travel documents and visas for Kenya and Tanzania before or during the trip where applicable.

Can I extend the safari after arriving in Tanzania?

Yes. Additional nights in Tanzania or other safari destinations can be arranged depending on your travel plans. Speak with our team when making your booking so we can help arrange an extension.
