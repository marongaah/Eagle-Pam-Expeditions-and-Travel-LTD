TOUR OVERVIEW

Traverse Mount Kenya via Sirimon and Chogoria

Experience Mount Kenya at its most spectacular on this 5-day traverse, combining two of the mountain's most scenic routes. Ascend gradually through forest and moorland via the Sirimon Route, acclimatizing at Old Moses Camp and Shipton's Camp before a pre-dawn summit push to Point Lenana.

After reaching the summit, descend via the dramatic Chogoria Route, passing Minto's Hut and the stunning Lake Michaelson before continuing through the Gorges Valley to Meru. Trekking with an experienced mountain guide, this route offers the best scenery Mount Kenya has to offer in one continuous adventure.

Sirimon–Chogoria Trekking Highlights

Point Lenana Summit

Reach Point Lenana at 4,985m, Mount Kenya's third-highest peak and the highest point accessible without technical climbing equipment.

Sirimon Route Ascent

Climb gradually through montane forest and open moorland on one of Mount Kenya's most scenic and gentle approach routes.

Lake Michaelson

Descend past the stunning glacial waters of Lake Michaelson, set beneath towering cliffs in the Gorges Valley.

Shipton's & Minto's Huts

Overnight at two of Mount Kenya's most iconic huts, offering spectacular views of the Central Peaks from either side of the mountain.

Expert Mountain Guide

Trek with an experienced, professional mountain guide who prioritizes your safety and acclimatization throughout the traverse.

Sunrise Summit Push

Set off before dawn for a summit attempt timed to reach Point Lenana as the sun rises over the Central Peaks.

DAY BY DAY

5-Day Sirimon–Chogoria Itinerary

A complete traverse of Mount Kenya, ascending via the Sirimon Route to Point Lenana and descending through the spectacular Chogoria Route to Meru.

Day 1

Nairobi → Nanyuki → Sirimon Gate → Old Moses Camp

Depart Nairobi in the morning and drive to Nanyuki, continuing to the Sirimon Gate at the edge of Mount Kenya National Park. Begin trekking through montane forest and bamboo zones, climbing steadily to Old Moses Camp on the edge of the moorland.

  • Morning departure from Nairobi
  • Drive to Nanyuki and Sirimon Gate
  • Trek through forest to the moorland zone
  • Arrive at Old Moses Camp (3,300m)
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 2

Old Moses Camp → Shipton's Camp

Trek across the open moorland of Mackinder's Valley, with spectacular views of the Central Peaks growing closer throughout the day. Arrive at Shipton's Camp in the afternoon to rest and acclimatize ahead of the summit attempt.

  • Breakfast at Old Moses Camp
  • Trek through Mackinder's Valley
  • Views of the Central Peaks
  • Arrive at Shipton's Camp (4,200m)
  • Early dinner and rest before summit night
Day 3

Summit Point Lenana → Descend to Minto's Hut (Chogoria Side)

Set off in the early hours for the summit push to Point Lenana (4,985m), timed to arrive for sunrise over the Central Peaks. After celebrating at the summit, begin the descent on the Chogoria side of the mountain towards Minto's Hut, passing dramatic volcanic scenery along the way.

  • Pre-dawn departure from Shipton's Camp
  • Summit Point Lenana for sunrise
  • Descend towards the Chogoria side
  • Arrive at Minto's Hut
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 4

Minto's Hut → Lake Michaelson → Chogoria Gate

Descend past the stunning Lake Michaelson, nestled beneath towering cliffs in the Gorges Valley. Continue through changing vegetation zones down to the Chogoria Gate before driving to Meru for the night.

  • Breakfast at Minto's Hut
  • Descend through the Gorges Valley
  • Views of Lake Michaelson
  • Reach Chogoria Gate
  • Drive to Meru for dinner and overnight stay
Day 5

Meru → Nairobi

After breakfast, depart Meru and travel back to Nairobi through the scenic Kenyan countryside, arriving in the afternoon with unforgettable memories of your Mount Kenya traverse.

  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Scenic drive back to Nairobi
  • Drop-off at hotel or airport
