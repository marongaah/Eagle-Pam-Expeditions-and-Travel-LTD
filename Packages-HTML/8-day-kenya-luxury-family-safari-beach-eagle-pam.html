TOUR OVERVIEW

Wildlife Encounters and Coastal Relaxation

This 8-day itinerary begins in Nairobi and heads to the Masai Mara via the Great Rift Valley. Spend time exploring the reserve on afternoon and full-day game drives, including a picnic lunch stop at the hippo pool. The journey then continues from the Mara through Nairobi to Mombasa for a beach stay before returning to Nairobi on Day 8.

The programme below follows the route, activities and meal plan shown in the TourRadar reference listing.
