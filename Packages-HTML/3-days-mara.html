Embark on an unforgettable three-day safari through Kenya's world-renowned Masai Mara National Reserve. Famous for its breathtaking landscapes, abundant wildlife, and the annual Great Migration, the Mara offers one of the finest safari experiences in Africa.

Enjoy thrilling game drives, witness lions, elephants, cheetahs, giraffes, zebras, and countless other species while staying in carefully selected safari lodges that combine comfort with authentic African hospitality.

DAY BY DAY

Safari Itinerary

Every day is carefully planned to give you the best wildlife experience while travelling comfortably across Kenya's most famous safari destination.

Day 1

Nairobi → Masai Mara

Pick up from your hotel or the airport before departing for Masai Mara through the scenic Great Rift Valley. Arrive at your lodge in time for lunch before enjoying your first afternoon game drive searching for lions, elephants, giraffes, zebras and many other animals.

  • Morning departure from Nairobi
  • Scenic Rift Valley stop
  • Lunch at the lodge
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner & overnight stay
Day 2

Full Day Game Drive

Spend the entire day exploring the vast plains of Masai Mara in search of the Big Five. Visit the Mara River during migration season and enjoy a picnic lunch in the wilderness.

  • Sunrise game drive
  • Big Five search
  • Mara River visit
  • Picnic lunch
  • Evening safari drive
Day 3

Masai Mara → Nairobi

Enjoy an early morning game drive before breakfast. Check out and begin your journey back to Nairobi with unforgettable safari memories.

  • Morning game drive
  • Breakfast
  • Return journey
  • Drop off in Nairobi
WHERE YOU STAY

Accommodation

Mara Sopa Lodge

Nestled on the slopes of the Oloolaimutia Hills, Masai Mara Sopa Lodge overlooks the Maasai Mara savannah. Blending authentic charm with modern comfort, this historic, eco-certified property features traditional African roundhouses, a swimming pool, and lush, mature gardens. Guests can easily access the reserve for wildlife viewing.
