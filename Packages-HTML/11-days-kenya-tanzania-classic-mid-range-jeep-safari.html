DAY BY DAY

11-Day Kenya & Tanzania Safari Itinerary

Journey from Kenya's famous safari destinations into Tanzania's spectacular northern circuit, experiencing incredible wildlife, breathtaking landscapes and unforgettable game drives throughout this 11-day adventure.

Day 1

Nairobi → Masai Mara

Depart Nairobi in the morning and travel through the scenic Great Rift Valley towards the legendary Masai Mara Game Reserve. Arrive in time for lunch before enjoying your first afternoon game drive across the Mara plains.

  • Morning departure from Nairobi
  • Scenic journey through the Great Rift Valley
  • Lunch and check-in at camp
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 2

Full Day Masai Mara Game Reserve

Spend a full day exploring the Masai Mara on morning and afternoon game drives. Search for lions, elephants, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras, cheetahs and other wildlife across the reserve's spectacular grasslands.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Breakfast at the camp
  • Full-day wildlife exploration
  • Picnic or camp lunch
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 3

Masai Mara → Lake Nakuru

After breakfast, depart the Masai Mara and travel towards Lake Nakuru National Park. Enjoy the changing scenery as you enter Kenya's Great Rift Valley and explore the Lake Nakuru area.

  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Depart Masai Mara
  • Scenic journey towards Lake Nakuru
  • Lake Nakuru game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 4

Lake Nakuru → Lake Naivasha

Explore the Lake Nakuru region before continuing to beautiful Lake Naivasha. Enjoy the surrounding Rift Valley scenery and wildlife before settling into your accommodation for the evening.

  • Morning breakfast
  • Lake Nakuru exploration
  • Depart for Lake Naivasha
  • Scenic Rift Valley landscapes
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 5

Lake Naivasha → Amboseli National Park

Depart Lake Naivasha and travel south towards Amboseli National Park. Arrive beneath the magnificent Mount Kilimanjaro and enjoy your first game drive in this famous elephant country.

  • Breakfast and departure
  • Journey towards Amboseli
  • Lunch en route
  • Arrival at Amboseli
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 6

Full Day Amboseli National Park

Spend the day exploring Amboseli National Park, famous for its enormous elephant herds and spectacular views of Mount Kilimanjaro. Enjoy morning and afternoon game drives through the park's diverse habitats.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Breakfast
  • Explore Amboseli National Park
  • Picnic or lodge lunch
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 7

Amboseli → Arusha, Tanzania

After breakfast, depart Amboseli and travel towards the Kenya-Tanzania border. Complete the border crossing before continuing to Arusha, Tanzania, the gateway to the country's famous northern safari circuit.

  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Depart Amboseli
  • Kenya-Tanzania border crossing
  • Continue to Arusha
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 8

Arusha → Lake Manyara

Leave Arusha and travel towards Lake Manyara National Park. Enjoy a game drive through the park, known for its beautiful landscapes, diverse birdlife, elephants and rich wildlife.

  • Breakfast in Arusha
  • Depart for Lake Manyara
  • Lake Manyara game drive
  • Wildlife viewing
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 9

Lake Manyara → Serengeti National Park

After breakfast, continue towards the legendary Serengeti National Park. Enter the vast plains of the Serengeti and enjoy an afternoon game drive as you begin exploring one of Africa's greatest wildlife destinations.

  • Breakfast and departure
  • Scenic journey towards Serengeti
  • Enter Serengeti National Park
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 10

Full Day Serengeti National Park

Spend a full day exploring the Serengeti on morning and afternoon game drives. Discover the park's vast plains and search for its incredible concentration of wildlife, including lions, elephants, cheetahs, buffaloes and wildebeest.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Breakfast
  • Full-day Serengeti exploration
  • Picnic or camp lunch
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 11

Serengeti → Ngorongoro Crater

Enjoy a morning game drive in the Serengeti before travelling towards the Ngorongoro Conservation Area. Continue through the spectacular highland scenery and arrive near the crater for your overnight stay, ready for your Ngorongoro experience.

  • Morning Serengeti game drive
  • Breakfast
  • Drive towards Ngorongoro
  • Scenic crater viewpoints
  • Dinner and overnight stay
