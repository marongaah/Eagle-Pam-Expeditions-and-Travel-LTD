TOUR OVERVIEW

Discover Kenya’s Wild and Beautiful North

Enjoy guided game drives, scenic journeys and opportunities to observe wildlife around lodges, waterholes and salt licks. This private, fully guided safari starts and ends in Nairobi.

DAY BY DAY

Your 7-Day Safari Itinerary

From the Aberdare highlands to the wild north and the beautiful wilderness of Meru.

DAY 1

Nairobi – Aberdare National Park

Depart Nairobi in the morning and travel through the Kikuyu farmlands towards the Aberdare highlands. Enjoy lunch at Aberdare Country Club before continuing to The Ark Lodge.

Spend the afternoon and evening watching wildlife from the lodge's viewing decks and balconies overlooking the waterhole and salt lick. The waterhole may be illuminated after dark, offering opportunities to observe visiting wildlife.

Meals: Lunch and dinner. Overnight: Aberdare area.

DAY 2

Aberdare – Mount Kenya Region

After breakfast, travel to the Mount Kenya region. Settle into your lodge and enjoy the surrounding forest and mountain scenery.

Depending on lodge arrangements and conditions, the afternoon offers opportunities for gentle nature walks and wildlife viewing around the waterhole, salt lick or photographic hide.

Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner. Overnight: Mount Kenya region.

DAY 3

Mount Kenya Region – Samburu National Reserve

Continue north to the striking landscapes of Samburu. After arriving and settling in, head out for an afternoon game drive as the light softens over the reserve.

Look out for northern Kenya wildlife such as Grevy's zebra, reticulated giraffe and gerenuk. The Samburu and Buffalo Springs area offers excellent opportunities for wildlife viewing and photography.

Meals: Breakfast and dinner. Overnight: Samburu area.

DAY 4

Full Day in Samburu National Reserve

Spend the day exploring Samburu on morning and afternoon game drives.

Search the reserve's varied habitats for its distinctive wildlife and enjoy time for birdwatching and photography. Return to the lodge between drives to relax and enjoy the surrounding wilderness.

Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner. Overnight: Samburu area.

DAY 5

Samburu – Meru National Park

Enjoy a final morning game-viewing opportunity in Samburu before travelling towards Meru National Park.

Arrive and settle into your accommodation, with time to appreciate the park's varied scenery and peaceful wilderness atmosphere.

Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner. Overnight: Meru area.

DAY 6

Full Day in Meru National Park

Explore Meru with morning and afternoon game drives. The park's mix of riverine areas, open grassland and woodland provides a varied setting for wildlife watching and photography.

Return to the lodge between drives to relax and enjoy the surroundings.

Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner. Overnight: Meru area.

DAY 7

Meru – Nairobi

Start the day with a final morning wildlife-viewing drive, followed by breakfast.

Begin the journey back to Nairobi. The listed itinerary indicates an expected hotel drop-off between approximately 16:30 and 17:30.

Meals: Breakfast. End of Safari: Nairobi.
