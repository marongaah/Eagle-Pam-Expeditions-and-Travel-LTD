This seven-day safari takes you through four distinctive wildlife regions of Kenya. Begin in the forested Aberdare highlands, continue to the Mount Kenya region, explore the rugged landscapes of Samburu and finish in the wilderness of Meru National Park.

Enjoy guided game drives, scenic journeys and opportunities to observe wildlife around lodges, waterholes and salt licks. This private, fully guided safari starts and ends in Nairobi.

Northern Kenya Safari Highlights

Aberdare Highlands

Travel through the Kikuyu farmlands into the forested Aberdare highlands and watch wildlife from the viewing decks of The Ark Lodge, overlooking a waterhole and salt lick.

Mount Kenya Region

Enjoy forest and mountain scenery with gentle nature walks and wildlife viewing around the waterhole, salt lick or photographic hide.

Samburu Wildlife

Explore Samburu National Reserve on morning and afternoon game drives, searching for northern Kenya species such as Grevy's zebra, reticulated giraffe and gerenuk.

Meru National Park

Discover Meru's mix of riverine areas, open grassland and woodland, with full-day game drives in a peaceful wilderness setting.

Birdwatching & Photography

Enjoy plenty of opportunities for birdwatching and wildlife photography across Samburu, Meru and the lodge waterholes along the route.

Private Guided Safari

Travel privately in a safari vehicle with an experienced English-speaking guide throughout your round-trip journey from Nairobi.

DAY BY DAY

7-Day Northern Kenya Safari Itinerary

Journey from the Aberdare highlands to the wild north and the beautiful wilderness of Meru, experiencing incredible wildlife, scenic landscapes and unforgettable game drives throughout this 7-day adventure.

Day 1

Nairobi → Aberdare National Park

Depart Nairobi in the morning and travel through the Kikuyu farmlands towards the Aberdare highlands. Enjoy lunch at Aberdare Country Club before continuing to The Ark Lodge for an afternoon and evening of wildlife viewing over the waterhole and salt lick.

  • Morning departure from Nairobi
  • Lunch at Aberdare Country Club
  • Transfer to The Ark Lodge
  • Waterhole and salt lick viewing
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 2

Aberdare → Mount Kenya Region

After breakfast, travel to the Mount Kenya region and settle into your lodge. Depending on lodge arrangements and conditions, enjoy gentle nature walks and wildlife viewing around the waterhole, salt lick or photographic hide.

  • Breakfast and departure
  • Drive to the Mount Kenya region
  • Forest and mountain scenery
  • Nature walks and wildlife viewing
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 3

Mount Kenya Region → Samburu National Reserve

Continue north to the striking landscapes of Samburu and head out for an afternoon game drive. Look out for northern Kenya wildlife such as Grevy's zebra, reticulated giraffe and gerenuk.

  • Breakfast and departure
  • Drive to Samburu
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Grevy's zebra, reticulated giraffe and gerenuk
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 4

Full Day Samburu National Reserve

Spend the day exploring Samburu on morning and afternoon game drives. Search the reserve's varied habitats for its distinctive wildlife and enjoy time for birdwatching and photography.

  • Morning game drive
  • Birdwatching and photography
  • Lunch and rest at the lodge
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 5

Samburu → Meru National Park

Enjoy a final morning game-viewing opportunity in Samburu before travelling towards Meru National Park. Arrive and settle into your accommodation, with time to appreciate the park's varied scenery.

  • Morning game viewing in Samburu
  • Breakfast and departure
  • Drive to Meru National Park
  • Lunch
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 6

Full Day Meru National Park

Explore Meru with morning and afternoon game drives. The park's mix of riverine areas, open grassland and woodland provides a varied setting for wildlife watching and photography.

  • Morning game drive
  • Riverine, grassland and woodland habitats
  • Lunch and rest at the lodge
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 7

Meru → Nairobi

Start the day with a final morning wildlife-viewing drive, followed by breakfast and the journey back to Nairobi, with hotel drop-off expected between 16:30 and 17:30.

  • Morning wildlife-viewing drive
  • Breakfast
  • Drive back to Nairobi
  • Hotel drop-off (16:30 - 17:30)
