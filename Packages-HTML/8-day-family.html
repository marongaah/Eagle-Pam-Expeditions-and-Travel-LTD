TOUR OVERVIEW

8-Day Classic Kenya Family 4x4 Safari

This 8-day private safari starts and ends in Nairobi and is designed for families and friends who want to see Kenya's classic wildlife destinations at a comfortable, low-intensity pace.

Travel through the Great Rift Valley to Lake Nakuru National Park, spend time on the shores of Lake Naivasha, then continue to the Masai Mara for three days of game drives across one of Africa's greatest wildlife reserves.

Travel exclusively with your own group and an experienced English-speaking guide in a 4x4 safari vehicle, with optional activities such as a boat trip, Hell's Gate visit and a sunrise hot air balloon safari.

DAY BY DAY

8-Day Classic Kenya Family Safari Itinerary

Start and end in Nairobi while visiting Lake Nakuru, Lake Naivasha and the Masai Mara on a private, family-friendly 4x4 safari.

Day 1

Nairobi

Begin your Kenya safari in Nairobi with breakfast and a relaxed first day. Settle in, meet your guide and, if you like, explore the city on an optional 4-hour Nairobi Experience tour.

  • Hotel pick-up in Nairobi
  • Breakfast
  • Optional 4-hour Nairobi city tour with lunch
  • Overnight in Nairobi
Day 2

Nairobi → Lake Nakuru

Drive through the scenic Great Rift Valley, passing Lake Naivasha and Lake Elementaita on the way to Lake Nakuru National Park for an afternoon game drive.

  • Great Rift Valley drive
  • Views of Lake Naivasha and Lake Elementaita
  • Lunch
  • Afternoon game drive in Lake Nakuru National Park
  • Dinner and overnight
Day 3

Lake Nakuru → Lake Naivasha

Start with an early morning game drive in Lake Nakuru National Park when wildlife is most active, then continue to Lake Naivasha.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Breakfast
  • Drive to Lake Naivasha
  • Lunch
  • Dinner and overnight
Day 4

Lake Naivasha

Enjoy a relaxed day at Lake Naivasha with optional activities, including a visit to Hell's Gate National Park, a boat trip across the lake and bike rental for the day.

  • Optional Hell's Gate National Park visit
  • Optional boat trip on Lake Naivasha (USD $30 per person)
  • Optional bike rental
  • Lunch, dinner and overnight
Day 5

Lake Naivasha → Masai Mara

After breakfast, travel to the Masai Mara National Reserve, arriving in time for lunch and an afternoon game drive.

  • Breakfast and departure
  • Drive to the Masai Mara
  • Lunch
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight
Day 6

Full Day Masai Mara National Reserve

Spend a full day exploring the Masai Mara on game drives. For an unforgettable experience, add an optional sunrise hot air balloon safari (pre-booking required).

  • Full day game drive
  • Optional sunrise hot air balloon safari (USD $450 per person)
  • Lunch
  • Dinner and overnight
Day 7

Full Day Masai Mara National Reserve

Enjoy another full day of game drives across the Masai Mara, with more chances to see its famous wildlife and landscapes.

  • Full day game drive
  • Lunch
  • Dinner and overnight
Day 8

Masai Mara → Nairobi

Enjoy a final sunrise game drive in the Masai Mara before breakfast and the journey back to Nairobi, arriving at your hotel in the late afternoon.

  • Sunrise game drive
  • Breakfast
  • Drive back to Nairobi
  • Hotel drop-off (16:30 - 17:30)
