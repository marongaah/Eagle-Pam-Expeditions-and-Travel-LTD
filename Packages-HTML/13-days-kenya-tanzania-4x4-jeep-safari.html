DAY BY DAY

13-Day Kenya & Tanzania Safari Itinerary

Journey from Kenya's famous savannahs into Tanzania's spectacular wilderness, experiencing incredible wildlife, breathtaking landscapes and unforgettable game drives throughout the 13-day adventure.

Day 1

Nairobi → Maasai Mara

Depart Nairobi in the morning and travel through the scenic Great Rift Valley towards the legendary Maasai Mara National Reserve. Arrive in time for lunch before heading out for your first afternoon game drive across the Mara's spectacular grasslands.

  • Morning departure from Nairobi
  • Scenic journey through the Great Rift Valley
  • Lunch and check-in at your camp or lodge
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 2

Full Day Maasai Mara Safari

Spend a full day exploring the Maasai Mara on morning and afternoon game drives. Search for lions, elephants, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras, cheetahs and other wildlife across the reserve's rich savannah ecosystem.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Breakfast at the camp or lodge
  • Full-day wildlife exploration
  • Picnic or lodge lunch
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 3

Maasai Mara → Lake Nakuru

After breakfast, depart the Maasai Mara and travel towards Lake Nakuru National Park. Enjoy the changing scenery as you journey through Kenya's Great Rift Valley before arriving at Lake Nakuru for your next safari experience.

  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Depart Maasai Mara
  • Scenic drive through the Rift Valley
  • Arrive in the Lake Nakuru area
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 4

Lake Nakuru → Lake Naivasha

Explore the Lake Nakuru region before continuing to beautiful Lake Naivasha. Enjoy the surrounding landscapes and wildlife before settling into your accommodation for the evening.

  • Morning breakfast
  • Lake Nakuru exploration
  • Depart for Lake Naivasha
  • Scenic Rift Valley landscapes
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 5

Lake Naivasha → Amboseli National Park

Depart Lake Naivasha and travel south towards Amboseli National Park. Arrive beneath the magnificent Mount Kilimanjaro and enjoy your first game drive in this famous elephant country.

  • Breakfast and departure
  • Journey towards Amboseli
  • Lunch en route
  • Arrival at Amboseli
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 6

Full Day Amboseli National Park

Spend the day exploring Amboseli National Park, famous for its enormous elephant herds and spectacular views of Mount Kilimanjaro. Enjoy morning and afternoon game drives through the park's diverse habitats.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Breakfast
  • Explore Amboseli National Park
  • Picnic or lodge lunch
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 7

Amboseli → Arusha, Tanzania

After breakfast, depart Amboseli and travel towards the Kenya-Tanzania border. Complete the border crossing before continuing to Arusha, Tanzania, the gateway to the country's famous northern safari circuit.

  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Depart Amboseli
  • Kenya-Tanzania border crossing
  • Continue to Arusha
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 8

Arusha → Lake Manyara

Leave Arusha and travel towards Lake Manyara National Park. Enjoy a game drive through the park, known for its beautiful landscapes, diverse birdlife, elephants and rich wildlife.

  • Breakfast in Arusha
  • Depart for Lake Manyara
  • Lake Manyara game drive
  • Wildlife viewing
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 9

Lake Manyara → Serengeti National Park

After breakfast, continue towards the legendary Serengeti National Park. Enter the vast plains of the Serengeti and enjoy an afternoon game drive as you begin your exploration of one of Africa's greatest wildlife destinations.

  • Breakfast and departure
  • Scenic journey towards Serengeti
  • Enter Serengeti National Park
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 10

Full Day Serengeti National Park

Spend a full day exploring the Serengeti on morning and afternoon game drives. Search the endless plains for lions, cheetahs, elephants, buffaloes, giraffes and other wildlife while experiencing the incredible landscapes of the Serengeti ecosystem.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Breakfast
  • Full-day Serengeti exploration
  • Picnic or lodge lunch
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 11

Serengeti → Ngorongoro Crater

Depart the Serengeti after breakfast and travel towards the Ngorongoro Conservation Area. Enjoy the spectacular scenery of the highlands before reaching the Ngorongoro area for your overnight stay.

  • Breakfast
  • Morning game drive
  • Depart Serengeti
  • Scenic journey to Ngorongoro
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 12

Ngorongoro Crater → Arusha

Enjoy an unforgettable crater experience before beginning your journey back towards Arusha. Descend into the Ngorongoro Crater for an incredible wildlife viewing experience before continuing to Arusha.

  • Early breakfast
  • Ngorongoro Crater game drive
  • Wildlife viewing
  • Depart for Arusha
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 13

Arusha → Nairobi

After breakfast, begin your final journey back to Nairobi. Cross the Kenya-Tanzania border and continue through the Kenyan countryside, arriving in Nairobi in the afternoon with unforgettable memories of your Kenya and Tanzania safari.

  • Breakfast
  • Check-out from accommodation
  • Travel towards the Kenya border
  • board shuttle bus
  • Continue to Nairobi
  • Drop-off at hotel or airport
