DAY BY DAY

Safari Itinerary

Experience Kenya's most iconic wildlife destinations on this exciting five-day camping safari travelling in a custom 4x4 Land Cruiser with professional safari guides.

Day 1

Nairobi → Masai Mara National Reserve

Depart Nairobi in the morning and travel through the spectacular Great Rift Valley towards the world-famous Masai Mara National Reserve. Arrive at your campsite in time for lunch before heading out on your first afternoon game drive in search of lions, elephants, giraffes, zebras and countless other wildlife.

  • Morning departure from Nairobi
  • Great Rift Valley viewpoint stop
  • Lunch at the campsite
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight camping
Day 2

Full Day Game Drive in Masai Mara

Spend a full day exploring the vast plains of the Masai Mara. Search for the Big Five, visit the Mara River, enjoy a picnic lunch in the reserve and witness the incredible diversity of wildlife that makes the Mara one of Africa's finest safari destinations.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Big Five wildlife viewing
  • Mara River visit
  • Picnic lunch
  • Evening return to camp
Day 3

Masai Mara → Lake Nakuru National Park

Enjoy breakfast before leaving Masai Mara for Lake Nakuru National Park. After arriving, enjoy an afternoon game drive in search of black and white rhinos, Rothschild giraffes, buffaloes and flamingos around the lake.

  • Breakfast at camp
  • Drive to Lake Nakuru
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Rhino sanctuary visit
  • Dinner and overnight camping
Day 4

Lake Nakuru → Lake Naivasha

After breakfast, depart for the beautiful Lake Naivasha, one of Kenya's few freshwater lakes. Spend the afternoon relaxing by the lakeshore or choose optional activities such as a boat ride among hippos and water birds or a walking safari on Crescent Island.

  • Breakfast at the campsite
  • Drive to Lake Naivasha
  • Optional boat safari
  • Optional Crescent Island walk
  • Dinner and overnight camping
Day 5

Lake Naivasha → Nairobi

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast before checking out of the campsite and beginning the journey back to Nairobi. Arrive in the afternoon with wonderful memories of Kenya's incredible wildlife, landscapes and unforgettable safari adventures.

  • Breakfast
  • Morning at leisure
  • Drive back to Nairobi
  • Arrival in the afternoon
  • End of safari services
