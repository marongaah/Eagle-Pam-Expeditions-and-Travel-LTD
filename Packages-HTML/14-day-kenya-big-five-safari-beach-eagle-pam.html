TOUR OVERVIEW

14 Days of Kenya Wildlife, Landscapes & Coastline

This 14-day Kenya adventure combines classic Big Five safari country with the country's beautiful Indian Ocean coast. Begin in Nairobi, travel to the Masai Mara for rewarding game drives, explore Lake Nakuru and Lake Naivasha, then continue south to Amboseli and Tsavo West before finishing with a relaxing stay at Diani Beach.

The itinerary below follows the route, day-by-day meal plan, accommodation names and listed activities of the TourRadar package used as the reference for this Eagle Pam Expeditions offering. Accommodation and operational arrangements should be reconfirmed at the time of booking.

DAY BY DAY

14-Day Safari Itinerary

Route, meals, accommodation and activities are presented according to the reference itinerary.

DAY 1

Arrival in Nairobi

Arrive at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport or meet your team at your Nairobi hotel between 06:30 and 07:30 as arranged. Transfer to your hotel and settle in. Optional visits include the Nairobi National Museum and the Giraffe Centre.

Meals: Not specified in the listing.
Accommodation: Maanzoni 680 Hotel (4-star).

DAY 2

Nairobi to Masai Mara National Reserve

Travel through the Great Rift Valley towards the world-famous Masai Mara. On arrival, enjoy a game drive in search of the reserve's remarkable wildlife.

Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner.
Accommodation: Jambo Mara Safari Lodge.
Included activity: Masai Mara game drive.

DAY 3

Full Day in the Masai Mara

Spend the day exploring the reserve on game drives. Look out for the Big Five and the many other species that inhabit the Mara ecosystem.

Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner.
Accommodation: Jambo Mara Safari Lodge.
Optional activities: Hot-air balloon safari and visit to a local Maasai village.

DAY 4

Masai Mara to Lake Nakuru National Park

Enjoy pre-breakfast sunrise wildlife viewing before departing for Lake Nakuru. After arriving and settling in, head out for an evening game drive in the park.

Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner.
Accommodation: Lake Nakuru Lodge.
Included activities: Sunrise game viewing and evening game drive.

DAY 5

Lake Nakuru to Lake Naivasha

Start with pre-breakfast game viewing at Lake Nakuru, then continue to Lake Naivasha. Enjoy a boat ride to see hippos and visit Crescent Island Game Sanctuary.

Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner.
Accommodation: Naivasha Sawela Lodge.
Included activities: Lake Naivasha boat ride and Crescent Island visit.

DAY 6

Lake Naivasha to Amboseli National Park

Travel south to Amboseli, known for its elephant herds and spectacular views of Mount Kilimanjaro. The listed programme includes an animal orphanage visit and an evening game drive.

Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner.
Accommodation: Amboseli Sopa Lodge.
Included activities: Animal orphanage visit and evening game drive.

DAY 7

Full Day in Amboseli National Park

Enjoy early morning wildlife viewing followed by afternoon game drives across Amboseli's varied habitats. An additional game drive to the swamp area after breakfast is listed as optional.

Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner.
Accommodation: Amboseli Sopa Lodge.
Optional activity: Additional swamp-area game drive.

DAY 8

Amboseli to Tsavo West National Park

Continue to Tsavo West, a dramatic landscape of volcanic hills, open plains and diverse wildlife. The itinerary includes an evening game drive, with Mzima Springs among the listed landmarks for the area.

Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner.
Accommodation: Ngulia Safari Lodge (name spelling to be reconfirmed).
Included activity: Evening game drive.

DAY 9

Tsavo West to Diani Beach

Depart Tsavo West and travel towards Kenya's Indian Ocean coast. Enjoy a game drive en route as listed, then arrive at Diani Beach and check in at your beach resort.

Meals: Breakfast, lunch and dinner.
Accommodation: Diani Reef Beach Resort.
Included activity: Game drive en route.

DAY 10

Diani Beach at Leisure

Enjoy a relaxed day by the Indian Ocean. The reference listing identifies resort facilities and optional leisure activities including swimming pool, beach activities, tennis, squash, table tennis, water aerobics, fitness centre, pool table, deep-sea fishing, water sports, volleyball, scuba diving, nature walks, children's club, cycling and birdwatching. These activities may carry additional charges.

Meals: Breakfast and dinner.
Accommodation: Diani Reef Beach Resort.

DAY 11

Diani Beach – Relax and Explore

Spend another day at leisure on the coast. Optional activities listed include water sports, beach activities, swimming and other watersports, subject to availability and local charges.

Meals: Breakfast and dinner.
Accommodation: Diani Reef Beach Resort.

DAY 12

Diani Beach – Coastal Leisure

Enjoy a free day to relax at the resort or arrange optional water sports and coastal experiences.

Meals: Breakfast and dinner.
Accommodation: Diani Reef Beach Resort.

DAY 13

Diani Beach – Final Full Day

Make the most of your final full day on the coast. Enjoy the beach, resort facilities or optional water sports at your own pace.

Meals: Breakfast and dinner.
Accommodation: Diani Reef Beach Resort.

DAY 14

Diani Beach to Nairobi – Departure

Enjoy breakfast before your return transfer from Diani Beach to Nairobi, bringing your Kenya safari and beach adventure to an end. Confirm the transfer method and connection schedule when booking.

Meals: Breakfast.
Accommodation: None – tour ends.
