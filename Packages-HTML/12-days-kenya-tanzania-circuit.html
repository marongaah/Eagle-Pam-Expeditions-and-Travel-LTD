TOUR OVERVIEW

12 Days Kenya & Tanzania Safari Circuit

Embark on an extraordinary 12-day safari adventure through Kenya and Tanzania, combining some of East Africa's most famous wildlife destinations into one unforgettable journey.

From the flamingos and wildlife of Lake Nakuru to the legendary plains of the Masai Mara, continue across the border into Tanzania for the endless savannahs of the Serengeti, the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater, Lake Manyara and Tarangire before returning to Kenya for an incredible Amboseli experience beneath Mount Kilimanjaro.

Travel in a comfortable safari vehicle with an experienced professional guide while enjoying exciting game drives, beautiful landscapes and exceptional opportunities to see Africa's iconic wildlife.

DAY BY DAY

12-Day Kenya & Tanzania Safari Itinerary

Experience an incredible East African safari circuit through Kenya and Tanzania, visiting iconic national parks and reserves filled with spectacular wildlife, dramatic landscapes and unforgettable safari experiences.

Day 1

Nairobi

Arrive in Nairobi and settle into your accommodation. Depending on your arrival time, enjoy some free time to relax or explore the city before beginning your exciting Kenya and Tanzania safari circuit.

  • Arrival in Nairobi
  • Airport or hotel transfer
  • Safari briefing
  • Overnight in Nairobi
Day 2

Nairobi → Lake Nakuru National Park

Depart Nairobi and travel through the scenic Great Rift Valley towards Lake Nakuru National Park. Enjoy a game drive through the park in search of rhinos, lions, buffaloes, giraffes and other wildlife.

  • Morning departure from Nairobi
  • Scenic Great Rift Valley drive
  • Lake Nakuru game drive
  • Wildlife viewing
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 3

Lake Nakuru → Masai Mara Game Reserve

After breakfast, depart Lake Nakuru and continue towards the legendary Masai Mara Game Reserve. Arrive in time for lunch followed by an afternoon game drive across the famous Mara plains.

  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Drive to Masai Mara
  • Lunch at the lodge or camp
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 4

Full Day Masai Mara Game Reserve

Spend a full day exploring the Masai Mara, one of Africa's greatest wildlife destinations. Enjoy extensive game drives across the reserve while searching for the Big Five and other wildlife.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Big Five wildlife viewing
  • Picnic or lodge lunch
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 5

Masai Mara → Serengeti National Park

Leave the Masai Mara and continue your journey into Tanzania towards the legendary Serengeti National Park. Cross the border and experience the changing landscapes as you enter one of Africa's most famous ecosystems.

  • Breakfast and departure
  • Border crossing into Tanzania
  • Continue towards Serengeti
  • Arrival and check-in
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 6

Full Day Serengeti National Park

Enjoy a full day of game drives across the vast Serengeti plains. Search for lions, leopards, cheetahs, elephants, buffaloes, giraffes and thousands of other animals.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Explore Serengeti plains
  • Wildlife and predator viewing
  • Picnic or lodge lunch
  • Afternoon game drive
Day 7

Serengeti → Ngorongoro Crater

Enjoy a morning game drive in the Serengeti before travelling towards the Ngorongoro Conservation Area. Arrive near the crater and enjoy the spectacular scenery surrounding this UNESCO World Heritage Site.

  • Morning Serengeti game drive
  • Breakfast
  • Drive towards Ngorongoro
  • Scenic crater viewpoints
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 8

Ngorongoro Crater → Lake Manyara

Descend into the magnificent Ngorongoro Crater for an exciting game drive before continuing towards Lake Manyara National Park. The crater offers an incredible concentration of wildlife within its dramatic volcanic landscape.

  • Early morning crater descent
  • Ngorongoro Crater game drive
  • Picnic lunch
  • Drive to Lake Manyara
  • Overnight near Lake Manyara
Day 9

Lake Manyara → Tarangire

Explore Lake Manyara before continuing to Tarangire National Park, famous for its ancient baobab trees, large elephant populations and diverse wildlife.

  • Morning Lake Manyara game drive
  • Breakfast
  • Drive to Tarangire
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 10

Tarangire → Amboseli National Park

After breakfast, depart Tarangire and cross back into Kenya towards Amboseli National Park. Enjoy spectacular views of Mount Kilimanjaro as you approach this famous elephant destination.

  • Breakfast and departure
  • Cross back into Kenya
  • Journey towards Amboseli
  • Arrival and check-in
  • Afternoon game drive
Day 11

Full Day Amboseli National Park

Spend the day exploring Amboseli National Park, famous for its magnificent elephant herds and spectacular views of Mount Kilimanjaro. Enjoy morning and afternoon game drives across the park.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Elephant viewing
  • Visit Observation Hill
  • Picnic or lodge lunch
  • Afternoon game drive
Day 12

Amboseli → Nairobi

Enjoy a final early morning game drive before breakfast and departure. Travel back to Nairobi with wonderful memories from your Kenya and Tanzania safari adventure.

  • Optional sunrise game drive
  • Breakfast at the lodge
  • Check-out
  • Drive back to Nairobi
  • Hotel or airport drop-off
