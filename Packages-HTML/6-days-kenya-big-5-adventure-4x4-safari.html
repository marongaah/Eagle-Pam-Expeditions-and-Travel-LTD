DAY BY DAY

6-Day Kenya Big 5 Safari Itinerary

Discover Kenya's most iconic wildlife destinations on a six-day 4x4 safari from Nairobi through Maasai Mara, Lake Nakuru and Amboseli National Park.

Day 1

Nairobi to Maasai Mara

Depart Nairobi in the morning and travel through the beautiful Kenyan countryside towards the world-famous Maasai Mara National Reserve. Enjoy scenic views along the journey before arriving in the Mara for lunch and your first exciting game drive.

  • Morning departure from Nairobi
  • Scenic journey towards Maasai Mara
  • Lunch at the camp or lodge
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 2

Maasai Mara

Spend a full day exploring the Maasai Mara on morning and afternoon game drives. Search for the legendary Big Five and experience the spectacular landscapes and abundant wildlife that make the Mara one of Africa's greatest safari destinations.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Breakfast at the camp or lodge
  • Full-day wildlife exploration
  • Lunch during the safari or at the lodge
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 3

Maasai Mara to Lake Nakuru

Enjoy breakfast before departing the Maasai Mara and travelling towards Lake Nakuru National Park. Continue through Kenya's scenic Rift Valley landscapes before arriving in Nakuru for your next safari experience.

  • Breakfast at the lodge or camp
  • Departure from Maasai Mara
  • Scenic drive through the Great Rift Valley
  • Arrival in Lake Nakuru
  • Game drive around Lake Nakuru National Park
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 4

Lake Nakuru to Amboseli National Park

After breakfast, depart Lake Nakuru and continue south towards Amboseli National Park. Travel through the Kenyan countryside before arriving at Amboseli, where you will enjoy your first opportunity to experience the park's famous elephants and spectacular Mount Kilimanjaro backdrop.

  • Breakfast at the lodge or camp
  • Depart Lake Nakuru
  • Scenic journey towards Amboseli
  • Arrival and lunch
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 5

Amboseli National Park

Spend the day exploring Amboseli National Park. Enjoy exciting game drives across the park's open plains while searching for large elephant herds, lions, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras and other wildlife. Weather permitting, enjoy magnificent views of Mount Kilimanjaro.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Breakfast at the camp or lodge
  • Wildlife exploration in Amboseli
  • Lunch at the lodge or camp
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner and overnight stay
Day 6

Amboseli to Nairobi

Enjoy an early morning game drive before breakfast and check-out. Depart Amboseli and travel back to Nairobi, arriving in the afternoon after an unforgettable Big Five safari adventure.

  • Optional early morning game drive
  • Breakfast at the camp or lodge
  • Check-out from Amboseli
  • Drive back to Nairobi
  • Drop-off at your hotel or airport

QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What can I expect to see on this 6-day Big 5 safari?

You will have opportunities to see some of Kenya's most famous wildlife, including elephants, lions, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras and, with luck, leopards and other predators. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed, as animals move freely in their natural environment.

Which destinations are included in the safari?

The safari covers three spectacular wildlife destinations: Maasai Mara National Reserve, Lake Nakuru National Park and Amboseli National Park.

What type of vehicle is used?

The safari is operated using a 4x4 safari vehicle, providing excellent visibility and comfortable access to Kenya's wildlife areas during game drives.

Where will we stay during the safari?

Accommodation is provided at Enkorok Camp in the Maasai Mara, Buraha Zenoni in Nakuru and Nyati Safari Camp in Amboseli, or similar accommodation of the same standard, subject to availability.

Is this safari suitable for families?

Yes. The safari is suitable for couples, families, solo travellers and small groups who want to experience Kenya's wildlife and landscapes on a multi-day adventure.

Are park entrance fees included?

Yes. The applicable entrance fees for Maasai Mara, Lake Nakuru and Amboseli National Park are included in the safari package.

Can the safari be customized?

Yes. Depending on your travel requirements, the safari can be discussed and customized where possible, including accommodation preferences, travel dates and additional experiences.
