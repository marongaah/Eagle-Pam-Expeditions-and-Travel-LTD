DAY BY DAY

Safari Itinerary

Experience Kenya's spectacular wildlife across the famous Masai Mara National Reserve and Lake Nakuru National Park on this exciting four-day budget safari.

Day 1

Nairobi → Masai Mara National Reserve

Depart Nairobi early in the morning and travel through the scenic Great Rift Valley with a stop at the viewpoint before continuing to Masai Mara. Arrive in time for lunch at camp before heading out for your first exciting afternoon game drive.

  • Pick up from Nairobi
  • Great Rift Valley viewpoint stop
  • Drive to Masai Mara
  • Lunch at the campsite
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner & overnight at camp
Day 2

Full Day Safari in Masai Mara

Spend the entire day exploring Kenya's most famous wildlife reserve. Search for the Big Five, visit the Mara River during migration season and enjoy a picnic lunch surrounded by spectacular African wilderness.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Big Five wildlife viewing
  • Mara River visit
  • Picnic lunch in the park
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner & overnight at camp
Day 3

Masai Mara → Lake Nakuru National Park

After breakfast, leave Masai Mara and travel to Lake Nakuru National Park. Enjoy an afternoon game drive in search of black and white rhinos, Rothschild giraffes, flamingos and many other bird species around the famous soda lake.

  • Breakfast at camp
  • Drive to Lake Nakuru
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Rhino sanctuary visit
  • Dinner & overnight near the park
Day 4

Lake Nakuru → Nairobi

Enjoy an early morning game drive for one final opportunity to spot wildlife before breakfast. Afterwards begin the journey back to Nairobi, arriving in the afternoon with unforgettable safari memories.

  • Morning game drive
  • Breakfast
  • Scenic drive to Nairobi
  • Arrival in the afternoon
  • End of safari

QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this safari suitable for first-time visitors?

Yes. This safari is perfect for first-time visitors to Kenya, combining two of the country's most famous national parks in just four unforgettable days.

Will we travel in a 4x4 Land Cruiser?

Yes. All game drives and transfers are carried out in a customized 4x4 safari Land Cruiser with a pop-up roof for excellent wildlife viewing.

Which animals can I expect to see?

Expect to see lions, elephants, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras, hyenas, cheetahs, rhinos, flamingos and many other incredible wildlife species.
