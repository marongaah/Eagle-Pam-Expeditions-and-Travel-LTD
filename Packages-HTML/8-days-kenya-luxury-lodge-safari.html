DAY BY DAY

Luxury Safari Itinerary

Explore Kenya's premier safari destinations while enjoying luxury accommodation, spectacular wildlife encounters and breathtaking scenery across Amboseli, Aberdare, Lake Nakuru and the Masai Mara.

Day 1

Nairobi → Amboseli National Park

Meet your safari guide in Nairobi before driving south to Amboseli National Park. Arrive in time for lunch at your luxury lodge before heading out on your first afternoon game drive beneath the magnificent backdrop of Mount Kilimanjaro.

  • Morning departure from Nairobi
  • Drive to Amboseli National Park
  • Lunch at the lodge
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner & overnight
Day 2

Full Day in Amboseli National Park

Spend the day exploring Amboseli on morning and afternoon game drives. Search for large elephant herds, lions, cheetahs, buffaloes, giraffes and countless bird species while enjoying spectacular views of Mount Kilimanjaro.

  • Early morning game drive
  • Big elephant herds
  • Lunch at the lodge
  • Afternoon safari
  • Luxury lodge overnight
Day 3

Amboseli → Aberdare National Park

After breakfast depart Amboseli and journey through Kenya's beautiful countryside to Aberdare National Park. Spend the evening watching wildlife gather at the floodlit waterhole from your unique safari lodge.

  • Breakfast
  • Drive to Aberdare
  • Lunch en route
  • Wildlife viewing from the lodge
  • Dinner & overnight
Day 4

Aberdare → Lake Nakuru National Park

Continue to Lake Nakuru National Park, famous for its thriving rhino population, Rothschild giraffes and spectacular birdlife. Enjoy an afternoon game drive before relaxing at your luxury lodge.

  • Breakfast
  • Drive to Lake Nakuru
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Rhino sanctuary
  • Overnight at the lodge
Day 5

Lake Nakuru → Masai Mara Game Reserve

Journey through the Great Rift Valley into the world- famous Masai Mara Game Reserve. Arrive for lunch followed by an exciting afternoon game drive in one of Africa's greatest wildlife destinations.

  • Breakfast
  • Drive to Masai Mara
  • Lunch at the lodge
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Dinner & overnight
Day 6

Full Day in Masai Mara Game Reserve

Spend a full day exploring the reserve in search of the Big Five while visiting the Mara River and enjoying a picnic lunch surrounded by nature.

  • Full-day game drive
  • Big Five search
  • Mara River visit
  • Picnic lunch
  • Luxury lodge overnight
Day 7

Full Day in Masai Mara Game Reserve

Enjoy another unforgettable safari through different sections of the Masai Mara, giving you more opportunities to photograph predators, elephants, giraffes and countless other wildlife species.

  • Morning safari
  • Photography opportunities
  • Picnic lunch
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Final overnight in the Mara
Day 8

Masai Mara Game Reserve → Nairobi

After breakfast depart the Masai Mara and travel back to Nairobi through the scenic Great Rift Valley, arriving in the afternoon where your unforgettable luxury safari comes to an end.

  • Breakfast
  • Check out
  • Drive to Nairobi
  • Rift Valley stop
  • End of safari

QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this safari private?

Yes. This luxury safari can be arranged as a private experience with your own professional safari guide and customized 4x4 Land Cruiser.

What wildlife can I expect to see?

You'll have excellent chances of seeing the Big Five, large elephant herds, giraffes, zebras, cheetahs, hyenas, hippos, crocodiles and hundreds of bird species across Kenya's top national parks.

When is the best time to travel?

This safari operates throughout the year. July to October is especially popular for the Great Migration, while January to March offers excellent wildlife viewing and clear views of Mount Kilimanjaro.
